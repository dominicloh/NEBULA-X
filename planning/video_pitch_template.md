# VIDEO PITCH TEMPLATE

The aim is to keep the final pitch under three minutes. If the pacing becomes crowded, simplify the explanations rather than exceeding the limit.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem and user

Provisional script:

- Rail engineers need a fast way to review subsystem sensor data and see which recordings deserve attention.
- They need evidence, not just a score.
- The shared app helps them work through two rail monitoring tasks from one place.

## 0:20–0:35 — Shared app and subsystem selector

Provisional script:

- The project provides one shared condition-monitoring app.
- Users choose Door or Rail Corrugation from a single interface.
- The workflow stays consistent while the underlying tasks remain separate.

## 0:35–1:20 — Door upload, segment results and download

Provisional script:

- Door uses a continuous motor-cycle stream.
- The app uploads the stream, inspects the signal, identifies cycle segments and classifies each cycle as Normal or Abnormal resistance.
- The output is a predicted segment table with the required time range and label.
- The engineer can review the evidence and download `door_predictions.csv`.

## 1:20–2:05 — Rail upload, classifications and download

Provisional script:

- Rail Corrugation uses file-level vibration and shock data.
- The app classifies each file as Normal, Side I or Side II.
- The engineer reviews file-level evidence and downloads `rail_predictions.csv`.

## 2:05–2:30 — Models, validation and explainability

Provisional script:

- Door and Rail each use their own validation and model logic.
- The app explains the evidence behind each result and the outputs are checked before download.
- The project keeps the data handling transparent and avoids over-claiming root causes.

## 2:30–2:40 — Closing value statement

Final closing idea:

“Our system turns complex rail sensor time series into clear, explainable and downloadable condition-monitoring results.”
