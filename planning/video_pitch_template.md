# VIDEO PITCH TEMPLATE

The aim is to keep the final pitch between 2 and 3 minutes. A target length of approximately 2 minutes 40 seconds leaves a small safety margin if the pacing is slightly longer than planned.

## 0:00–0:25 — Problem

Provisional script:

- Modern trains produce large amounts of bogie-temperature telemetry.
- Important developing abnormalities may be hidden among normal readings.
- Engineers have limited time and cannot inspect everything manually.

## 0:25–0:45 — User

Provisional script:

- The primary user is the rail condition-monitoring or reliability engineer.
- They need to identify which train component requires attention, understand whether its condition is worsening, and decide whether the issue should be investigated.

## 0:45–1:10 — Solution

Provisional script:

- We analyse bogie-temperature telemetry to detect current and developing abnormal heating patterns.
- We use per-component baselines where possible and monitor the recent trend of each relevant signal.
- We group abnormal readings into meaningful events and prioritise the most relevant components for review.
- We present the evidence in a dashboard so the engineer can understand the alert and the likely urgency.

## 1:10–2:05 — Dashboard demonstration

Shot list:

1. Open fleet overview
2. Point to the highest-priority component
3. Select an alert
4. Show the temperature trend
5. Show the expected baseline or range
6. Show when the abnormal behaviour started
7. Show why the model flagged the event
8. Briefly show filters or component navigation

### Suggested on-screen callouts

- Current abnormal behaviour
- Developing trend
- Supporting evidence
- Priority ranking
- Engineer review decision

Final closing idea:

“Instead of asking engineers to inspect more data, our system helps them understand which bogie component deserves attention and why. Every train is already telling us what it needs.”

This wording can be refined slightly, but it should remain natural and not overly dramatic.
